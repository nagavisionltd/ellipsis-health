# Remix of Ellipsis Launchpad

Build a premium one-page landing website for 'Ellipsis Health – The 2.0 Version of You Masterclass' led by Dr Jack Darby. Style: Apple x Nike x MasterClass. Dark premium aesthetic with white, charcoal, emerald and gold accents. Cinematic full-screen hero with subtle animated particles and soft gradients. Sections: Hero with headline 'Unlock the 2.0 Version of You', compelling subheading about affordable transformation, CTA buttons 'Reserve Your Seat' and 'View Curriculum'. Add trust badges (Doctor, Sports Science, Interactive, Limited to 50 Seats). Curriculum section with four premium cards: Neurochemical Mastery & Mental Optimisation; Physical Optimisation & Lifestyle Levers; Mental Toughness & Spiritual Growth; Integration & Daily Protocol Design. Include an 'Why This Works' section with icons for Practical, Affordable, Science-Based, Interactive. About Dr Jack timeline highlighting medical doctor, Loughborough sports science graduate, PT/nutrition expert, transformed from 105kg to 80kg, entrepreneur, advisor. Include inspirational quote: 'Adversity is the forge by which we grow. Pain is not your enemy, but your teacher.' Benefits section with cards for 2-month coaching, free book, MediFit Retreat giveaway worth £7,500, complete protocol. Pricing section showing £100 investment, 3-hour live session, limited to 50 seats. Include FAQ, testimonials placeholders, countdown timer placeholder, sticky CTA, smooth scrolling, premium animations, responsive design, accessible typography. Footer branded Ellipsis Health and MediFit. Use modern glassmorphism sparingly, elegant spacing, world-class conversion-focused layout.
